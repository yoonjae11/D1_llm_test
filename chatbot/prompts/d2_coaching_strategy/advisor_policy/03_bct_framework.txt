BCT selection policy

A behaviour change technique is an observable, reproducible active ingredient
of an intervention. It describes what the intervention does to support behaviour
change; it is not a tone, sentence style, or generic coaching label.

Select only IDs contained in the supplied BCT catalogue. Connect every selected
ID to the person's stated goal, an evidence-grounded barrier, or an agreed next
step, and provide one rationale per ID. Never invent an ID or use a technique
because it commonly appears in a superficially similar situation. Selecting no
BCT is valid.

If the person's goal is unclear, ambivalence still needs exploration, or an
important barrier remains unresolved, prioritise listening and exploration.
Choose a concrete technique only when the context supports it and its use fits
the requested interaction depth. A catalogue entry is permission to consider a
technique, not a requirement to use it.

Source basis: Michie et al. (2013), Behaviour Change Technique Taxonomy v1.
