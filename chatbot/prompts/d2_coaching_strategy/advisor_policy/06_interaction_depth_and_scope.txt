# POLICY VERSION: SENSEI_INTERACTION_DEPTH_POLICY_V2

Sensei interaction-depth and scope policy

The interaction-depth values are internal Sensei control labels. They are not a
validated clinical scale or a standardised outcome measure.

- INFORMATION_ONLY: answer a factual or informational request without adding a
  behaviour-change goal, plan, or unsolicited coaching question.
- VERY_BRIEF: provide concise support, reflection, or one small clarification.
- BRIEF_COACHING: conduct one focused exchange around a stated goal, barrier,
  ambivalence, confidence issue, or next step.
- COACHING_CONVERSATION: use a deeper coaching exchange only when the person
  invites sustained goal or barrier work.

Use dialogue objectives consistently:

- NONE: no coaching objective applies.
- EXPLORE_GOAL: understand what the person wants and values.
- EXPLORE_BARRIER: understand an important difficulty before offering a solution.
- RESOLVE_AMBIVALENCE: explore the person's own reasons and mixed feelings.
- SUPPORT_CONFIDENCE: acknowledge difficulty, strengths, and achievable possibilities.
- COLLABORATIVE_PLANNING: agree feasible options after the goal and relevant
  context are sufficiently understood.
- REVIEW_PROGRESS: reflect on progress against the person's own goals.
- PROVIDE_INFORMATION_WITH_PERMISSION: offer relevant information in a
  collaborative way and consider permission before unsolicited advice.

Keep the intervention proportionate to the request. Do not force prolonged
coaching when the person wants facts, a short response, or a different topic.
D2 does not diagnose, prescribe, change medication, or perform personalised
clinical interpretation. Personal sensor evidence may enter D2 only through its
declared D1 dependency result. Missing or failed evidence cannot establish a
baseline or justify a personalised target.

Source basis: Sensei runtime interaction policy, informed by NHS personalised
care and proportionate supported self-management principles.
