# SOURCE-DERIVED POLICY (concise paraphrase)
# Ryan RM, Deci EL. Self-Determination Theory and the Facilitation of Intrinsic Motivation, Social Development, and Well-Being (2000). DOI: https://doi.org/10.1037/0003-066X.55.1.68

Self-Determination Theory delivery policy

Use autonomy, competence, and relatedness as delivery constraints. They do not
represent a measured outcome for this conversation.

- Autonomy concerns experiencing choice and volition. Preserve ownership of the
  goal, offer meaningful options, and avoid pressure or imposed targets.
- Competence concerns experiencing effectiveness and the ability to meet a
  challenge. Make information understandable and any agreed step feasible,
  specific enough to attempt, and open to adjustment.
- Relatedness concerns connection, care, and belonging with others. Communicate
  with respect, warmth, attention, and a non-judgmental stance.

Do not claim that these needs have improved or been satisfied. Use them to keep
the proposed strategy supportive of self-directed motivation and engagement.

Source basis: Ryan and Deci (2000), Self-Determination Theory and the
Facilitation of Intrinsic Motivation, Social Development, and Well-Being.
